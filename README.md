## 📖 About the Book · 关于本书

*Streams in the Desert* (荒漠甘泉) is a beloved classic devotional by **L. B. Cowman**, first published in 1925. Born out of Mrs. Cowman's own experiences of hardship and faith during years of missionary work in Japan and China, the book is a masterful collection of meditations, Scripture, and inspirational writings that has sustained and encouraged readers for a century.

Each short daily entry speaks with quiet wisdom to the universal human experience — struggle, hope, loss, perseverance, and renewal. Whether or not you hold a religious faith, the book's reflections on inner strength, patience, and the beauty found in difficult seasons resonate deeply across cultures and generations.

《荒漠甘泉》由考文夫人（L.B. Cowman）著，首版于1925年，是百年来感动无数读者的经典励志灵修读本。全书每日一篇，文字隽永，充满人生智慧与内心力量。无论读者是否有宗教信仰，皆能从中找到共鸣与安慰。

## 🚀 How to Use · 使用方法

1. Click any link above to open the reader in your browser · 点击上方链接，在浏览器中打开读本
2. Select the month/week from the dropdown menu · 从下拉菜单选择月份或周次
3. Use the section tabs to navigate daily entries · 点击标签切换每日内容
4. Tap any **highlighted word** to see its definition · 点击高亮词汇查看释义
5. Press 🎧 to play the audio narration for each section · 点击音频栏播放朗读
6. Toggle 🌙 for night mode · 切换夜间模式
7. Use A− / A+ to adjust font size · 调整字体大小

**On mobile (iPhone / Android):**
Swipe **left** to switch to Chinese · Swipe **right** to switch to English
向左滑动切换中文 · 向右滑动切换英文
